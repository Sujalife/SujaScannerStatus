Prompt set: premium semi-realistic sculpted 3D workplace artwork with a green, white and deep-purple palette, mint highlights, soft cinematic lighting, crisp readable forms, no people, no mock interface panels or watermarks. Hub hero uses the exact title “SUJA LIFE TOOLS” above a hexagonal command emblem with green juice bottles, produce crates, a clipboard, tools, and subtle warehouse logistics. Dock hero uses the exact title “SUJA DOCK STATUS” above a warehouse with bays 16–19, white freight trailers, boxed juice on blue pallets and status beacons. Hub icon is a green-purple leaf emblem in a white hexagonal toolkit hub rim against forest green. Dock icon is a white freight trailer at a purple-trimmed loading dock with a green beacon in a lime hexagonal rim. Shared background is a quiet forest-green warehouse atmosphere with peripheral leaves, purple beams and bottling/warehouse details, leaving the center clear for app content.

Scanner Status assets extend the native vector icon family with a scanner illustration and green/purple sharing card. SVG sources are retained; PNG share art and 192/512px icons are deterministic raster exports. No Runway credits or external image-generation service were used.
